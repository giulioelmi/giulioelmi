# Hey, I'm Giulio 👋

I grew up in Molveno, a small mountain village in the Italian Dolomites. Now I build AI products in Los Angeles.

I'm finishing an M.S. in Business Analytics at UCLA Anderson and working as an AI Product Manager intern at Keck Medicine of USC. Most of what I build starts with a messy, expensive problem: patients stuck in the hospital waiting on paperwork, clinic slots lost to no-shows, a public weather forecast that runs a couple of degrees cold. I like taking those from a whiteboard sketch to something people use every week.

## Things I've built

| Project | What it does | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| [TransferAI](https://github.com/giulioelmi/transfer-ai) | An AI agent that runs a hospital patient transfer end to end. It reads the chart, picks the receiving hospital, fills in that hospital's forms, calls the transfer center to answer its questions, and faxes the packet. | TypeScript, FHIR, Twilio voice |
| [LUMINA](https://github.com/giulioelmi/LUMINA) | A Perplexity-style search engine. Answers stream in with clickable citations from the live web and your own documents, and harder questions get split into sub-questions that are researched separately. | JavaScript, MongoDB, RAG |
| [Kalshi weather trading](https://github.com/giulioelmi/kalshi-weather-trading) | An XGBoost model that corrects the cold bias in public temperature forecasts for LA's daily high, then trades the mispriced contracts on Kalshi. The historical backtest shows about +11¢ of average edge. | Python, XGBoost, NOAA data |
| [SeeWeeS ops agents](https://github.com/giulioelmi/Meta_AI_Agents_Final) | A multi-agent system for a specialty medicine distributor that weighs shipments, SLAs, weather risk, and capacity when planning dispatch, checks its own output, and runs what-if scenarios. | Python, LangGraph |
| [Surf lineup detector](https://github.com/giulioelmi/surf-lineup-detector) | A YOLO model trained on 200 images that counts surfers in a live CCTV feed. | Python, YOLO |
| [Yelp + NOAA pipeline](https://github.com/giulioelmi/data-management-final) | A one-command data pipeline that joins Yelp reviews with weather records and feeds a Tableau dashboard. | Python, PySpark, Tableau |
| [NFL Big Data Bowl](https://github.com/giulioelmi/NFL_BDB) | Our UCLA MSBA team's entry to the NFL's player-tracking analytics competition. | Python, Jupyter |

## At work (code is private, results aren't)

- Built an AI prototype that predicts hospital discharge delays before they happen. It found up to $2M in recoverable revenue and won Keck's company hackathon.
- Led the engineering and data science team on a clinic no-show model (XGBoost with SMOTE). I pitched the ~$500K annual ROI case to the CIO, and the project was approved for development.
- Built six AI agents in Microsoft Copilot Studio. The three department-wide ones are used weekly by more than 40% of a 400-person IT department.
- Benchmarked how GPT, Claude, and Gemini talk about Keck versus UCLA Health and Cedars-Sinai across 40 healthcare questions, using an LLM judge. Marketing leadership changed its content strategy based on the results.
- Built a SharePoint system that tracks 500+ software applications and $5M+ in yearly spend, and nudges owners when renewals come up.

## Before tech

I founded Activity Trentino, an alpine tourism startup back home. I polled 150 hotels before building anything, set up a partnership with the regional tourism authority, and grew it to 100+ partner hotels and $100K+ in first-year revenue.

More recently I built [Jobbamboo](https://jobbamboo.com) after talking to classmates who kept missing job postings. It watches 500+ company career pages and sends an alert within 30 minutes of a new posting. It has 100+ active users.
